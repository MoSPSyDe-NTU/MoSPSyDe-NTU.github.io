        <div id="pd-opportunities">We currently do <b>not</b> have open positions for postdoctoral positions but highly encourage you to apply for <b>scholarship/fellowship programmes</b>. Please <b>contact Ken first</b> if you are interested in any postdoctoral experience at MoSPSyDe.</div>

        ---
        
        ### Available Scholarships

        **1. [Lee Kuan Yew Postdoctoral Fellowship (LKYPDF)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/lee-kuan-yew-postdoctoral-fellowship-(lkypdf))**  
        - **Eligibility:** Recent PhD graduates, or with at most 1 postdoctoral training from a reputable university  
        - **Timeline:** Typically Nov–Feb (Currently closed)

        **2. [Presidential Postdoctoral Fellowship (PPF)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/presidential-postdoctoral-fellowship-(ppf))**  
        - **Eligibility:** Within 5 years of obtaining PhD or equivalent degree  
        - **Timeline:** TBA

        **3. [The Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/the-eric-and-wendy-schmidt-ai-in-science-postdoctoral-fellowship)**   
        - **Eligibility:** Within 3 years of having obtained PhD degree or equivalent degree in non-computer science areas  
        - **Timeline:** Typically December

        **4. [NTU AI-for-X Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-careers/ntu-ai-for-x-postdoctoral-fellowship)**   
        - **Eligibility:** PhD conferred within the past 3 years in a STEM field  
        - **Timeline:** Typically October

        **5. [SG Academies South-East Asia Fellowship](https://snas.org.sg/aboutsaseaf)**   
        - **Eligibility:** ASEAN citizen (non-Singapore citizen or PR) with PhD  
        - **Timeline:** Typically August

        **6. External fellowship from your country**   
        - Many countries support their PhD graduates to pursue their postdoc overseas, including NTU. We strongly recommend to check these opportunities first. Please also check [this website](https://ecrcentral.org/fundings/) to see the comprehensive list of available fellowship programmes. 
        - **Examples:**
          - From EU: [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)
          - From Japan: [JSPS Overseas Research Fellowships](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-ab/)

      title: 'PhD Student Positions'
      text: |
        <div id="phd-opportunities">NTU has two intakes annualy: <b>August</b> and <b>January</b>. The deadline is usually five months prior to the admission.</div>

        - All students need to fulfil the [admission requirements](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cceb/admissions/detail/scbe-doctor-of-philosophy-(phd)-(by-research)#programme) at CCEB.
        - All applicants (except for self-funded students) are strongly encouraged to apply for NTU scholarships (you need to declare in the application).
        - Prospective students may consider external scholarship opportunities or self-funding since funded positions are very limited.
          - [A*STAR Graduate Scholarship](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/scholarships/home/scholarships/ags--scholarship): carefully read the website since eligibility is limited.
          - External graduate scholarship from your home countries or industrial funding
          - Please check [here](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/financialmatters/pgtuitionfees) to consider a self-funding option.
          - Please also check [this website](https://ecrcentral.org/fundings/) to see the comprehensive list of available fellowship programmes.
        - Please <b>contact Ken in advance</b> if you want to apply.

        <div id="master-opportunities">Three different programmes are available to join our research group as NTU master students. Please note that both programmes are self-funded (check tuition fee etc in advance).

          - Please contact Ken in advance if you want to apply.
        - [Master of Science in Chemical Engineering by Coursework](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cceb/admissions/detail/master-of-science-in-chemical-engineering)
          - While this programme mainly focuses on courses, you may choose a research project (CH6300) under our group's supervision.
          - Please contact Ken in advance to show your interests so that we can call some projects.
        - [Master of Science in Chemical Modelling by Coursework](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cceb/admissions/detail/master-of-science-chemical-modelling)
          - While this programme mainly focuses on courses, you may choose a research project (CH6550/6551) under our group's supervision.
          - Please contact Ken in advance to show your interests so that we can call some projects.

      text: |
        <div id="ug-opportunities">While NTU undergraduate students have opportunities to join our research group, possible programmes and eligibilities highly depend on their courses. The examples are as follows:

        - [Independent Research Project (CH4702 / CH4703)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cceb/admissions/undergraduates/course-information)
          - Major Prescribed Elective (MPE) to 3rd – 4th Year CBE students and as Broadening and Deepening Elective (BDE) to 2nd - 4th Year CCEB (BIE/CBE/CHEM) students
          - 1 semester
        - [Undergraduate Research Experience on Campus (URECA)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/undergraduate-research-experience-on-campus-(ureca))
          - 2nd - 4th Year NTU undergraduate students
          - Great opportunities if you are interested in graduate (master's or PhD) programmes
        - [Final Year Project](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cceb/admissions/undergraduates/final-year-project)
          - 2 semester projects for BIE students
          - Opportunities may be limtied due to eligible research topics
        - [Summer research programme](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/cceb/admissions/undergraduates/undergraduate-research)
          - For CHEM students
          - Opportunities may be quite limited due to eligible research topics

        If you are interested, please send your CV and research interest to Ken via email, <b>clarfying under which programme you want to join</b>.

        <div id="visiting">We are always welcome for visiting researchers regardless of levels and visiting durations. Interested candidates are welcome to send their CVs and motivation letters (indicating the purpose, timeline, and grant situations) to Prof. Ken. <b>Please also carefully check the followings to make sure which programmes apply to your case.</b>

          - Please check the [website](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/student-exchanges/visiting-research) to see more details, including fees (note that research fees are required for long-term stay).
          - Please secure scholarships/grants by yourself or consider self-funded.
        - [Global Connect Fellowship](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/about-us/global/global-connect-fellowship)
          - Summer research opportunities for bachelor's and master's students graduating in this or next year
          - Application period is around mid-Jan to mid-Feb.
        - [Exchange programmes](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/student-exchanges)
          - Some universities are making agreements on exhcange programmes at NTU.
          - Please check available options and eligibilities [here](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/student-exchanges).
